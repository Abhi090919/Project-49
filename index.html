ANXA Infra · Project 49 · Hyderabad

Same city. Different lives. Why should the homes be the same?

Project 49 by ANXA Infra designs and builds individual homes for families in Hyderabad, on their own land. Built around a family, not around a floor plan. 49 homes. 7 ideas. No repetition.

How we build your home

  1. It begins with your land

    Every great home starts with its ground. We walk it with you first: the light, the trees, the way the ground falls.

  2. Then, a philosophy

    You choose the philosophy your home grows from: light, air, art, roots, earth, silence or future. Then it is drawn exclusively for your land and your family alone.

  3. And finally, your home

    We design it, build it and hand you the keys. A home that is yours alone, and stays one of one.

Seven design philosophies

Each philosophy is a way of living in a home, not a fixed plan. Yours is shaped around your land, your family and the way you want to live.

  • Light

    What if sunlight became a building material? Rooms are placed and openings cut so daylight moves through the house from morning to evening, and shade arrives where the afternoon is hottest.

  • Air

    What if a home could breathe? Cross-ventilation, courtyards and deep openings let the breeze pass through every room, so the house stays cool the way Deccan homes always have.

  • Art

    What if the house itself became the artwork? Gallery walls, murals and space for sculpture are designed into the house from the first drawing, not hung on it afterwards.

  • Roots

    What if the future remembered where it came from? Carved stone, the threshold and the courtyard: houses made by hand, in the building language of the Deccan.

  • Earth

    What if landscape became infrastructure? Rammed-earth walls, natural stone and trees growing inside the house. Materials taken from the land and built back into it.

  • Silence

    What if silence became the ultimate luxury? Thick walls, water and quiet courts. Each house is planned so the noise of the city stops at the door.

  • Future

    What if a home could evolve with the people inside it? Smart glass, hidden screens and a kitchen that grows food, built in so technology is there when you need it and gone when you do not.

Where we build: the green west of Hyderabad

Where there is still room for a garden and a tree, and the city is close: Kokapet, Narsingi, Gandipet, Tellapur, Moinabad, the Financial District, Jubilee Hills and Banjara Hills.
