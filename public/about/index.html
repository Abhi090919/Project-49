[ About ANXA Infra ]

One team, drawing to handover.

ANXA Infra (anxainfra.com) is a B2B construction and infrastructure services company. We design, engineer and build for developers, enterprises, industrial operators and public bodies, from our headquarters in Hyderabad and on sites across India. The people who draw your building are the people who build it.

[ Questions people ask ]

01

What is ANXA Infra?

+

ANXA Infra (anxainfra.com) is a B2B construction and infrastructure services company headquartered in Hyderabad, India, and working across India. It designs, engineers and builds residential and commercial buildings, industrial and manufacturing facilities, data centres and mission-critical infrastructure, and railway and transportation infrastructure.

02

Is anxainfra the same as ANXA Infra?

+

Yes. anxainfra is the web address and short name of ANXA Infra, the construction and infrastructure company. It is often written as ANXA Infra, Anxa Infra or anxainfra.

03

Who does ANXA Infra work for?

+

Developers, enterprises, industrial operators and public bodies who need one team to take a project from the first soil test to final handover.

04

What services does ANXA Infra offer?

+

Design, engineering and construction under one roof (EPC and design-build): structural design, site works, civil and structural construction, steel structures, heavy lifts, finishing and handover.

05

Which industries does ANXA Infra build for?

+

Four: residential and commercial buildings; industrial and manufacturing facilities; data centres and mission-critical infrastructure; railways and transportation infrastructure.

06

Where does ANXA Infra work?

+

Headquartered in Hyderabad, Telangana, with projects across India.

07

What is Project 49?

+

Project 49 is ANXA Infra's own real estate venture: 49 one-of-a-kind homes in Hyderabad, built around seven design philosophies, with no two homes alike. It has its own website at anxainfra.com/project-49.

08
