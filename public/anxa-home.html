[ 01 ]  About ANXA

One team from the first drawing to the last brick.

At ANXA Infra, the people who draw your building are the people who build it. Architects, engineers and site teams work as one, so every plan is made for the ground it stands on and every site works from drawings it helped shape.

We work for developers, enterprises, industrial operators and public bodies. You get a project that stays on plan and on budget, and one partner who answers for all of it, from the first soil test to final handover.
