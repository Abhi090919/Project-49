What we do

We plan. We build. We deliver.

Our drawings are made by people who know what a site can deliver, and our site teams work from drawings they helped make buildable.

01

Project design

Concept, space planning, 3D views, elevations, working and GFC drawings, interiors, and approval sets for GHMC, HMDA, BBMP and BDA.

02

Site evaluation

Site conditions, access, excavation, movement constraints, material storage, safety and readiness, assessed before work begins.

03

Cost & BOQ

Bills of quantity, specifications and budgets across materials, labour, RCC, masonry, steel, equipment and external works.

04

Construction engineering

Excavation, RCC, columns, beams, slabs, masonry, plastering, waterproofing, retaining walls, tanks and heavy-duty flooring.

05

Construction management

Daily supervision and dependable manpower, holding civil, steel, MEP, interior and specialist vendors to one schedule.

06

Heavy equipment & steel

Crane loading and unloading, heavy steel handling, machinery movement, and equipment foundations to OEM tolerances.
