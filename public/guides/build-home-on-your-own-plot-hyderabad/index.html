Building a home on your own plot
in Hyderabad, step by step.

By ANXA Infra · Project 49 · Updated October 2026

You own the land. Now you want a home on it that is built for your family, not picked from a catalogue. This is the sequence most independent homes in Hyderabad follow, and what to get right at each stage.

1. Start with the land, not the floor plan

Before any drawing, understand the plot itself: which way it faces, where the morning and afternoon sun falls, which trees are worth keeping, how the ground slopes and where rainwater runs. In Hyderabad's heat, the orientation of the house decides how comfortable it will be for decades.

  • Walk the plot at different times of day.
  • Note the road side, neighbours, views and noise.
  • Keep good trees; they are shade you cannot buy later.

2. Check the paperwork and the ground

Make sure the title, survey boundaries and land use are clear, and that the plot falls in the layout you expect. Then get a soil test, because the soil decides the foundation design and part of the cost.

  • Clear title and link documents.
  • A boundary survey with the plot marked on site.
  • A soil investigation for the structural engineer.

3. Decide how your family wants to live

List who lives in the house now and in ten years: parents, children, guests, work from home, prayer, hobbies. Decide what matters most to you, such as light, cool air, quiet, a garden or space for art. This brief is what a good architect designs from.

4. Design: concept, plans and structure

The architect turns the brief and the land into a concept, then detailed floor plans, elevations and sections. A structural engineer designs the foundation, columns and slabs. Services (plumbing, electrical, water storage, solar) should be planned now, not added later.

5. Building permission

Independent houses in Hyderabad need building permission from the local authority (GHMC, HMDA or the relevant municipality, depending on where the plot is) before construction starts. The architect prepares the drawings to the applicable building rules, including setbacks and height.

6. Construction

Construction runs from foundation and structure to walls, roofing, services, plastering, flooring, joinery and finishes. Regular site visits by the architect and a clear schedule keep the house true to the design.

7. Handover and living in

Before moving in, check every room, fitting and service, and collect the drawings, warranties and maintenance notes. A home built around your family should feel right from the first morning.

Quick answers

Do I need my own land to build with ANXA Infra?

Yes. Project 49 homes are designed and built on the client's own plot in Hyderabad.

What is the first step to build a house on my plot?

Study the land: orientation, sun, trees, slope and access. Then check the title and get a soil test before design starts.

Who gives building permission for a house in Hyderabad?

The local authority where the plot is located, such as GHMC, HMDA or the municipality, based on drawings prepared to the applicable building rules.
